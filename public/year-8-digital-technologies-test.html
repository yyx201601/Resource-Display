Q8. Data Interception (2 marks)

Mia sends a private message across a public network. An attacker copies the data while it is travelling. The copied message looks like 8F#1 XQ!7 92@.

Explain what happened and why the message may still be protected.

Think about: interception · encryption · what the attacker can understand

Q9. Phishing / Scam (2 marks)

Alex receives a message saying: “Your school account will be deleted today. Click this link and sign in now.” The sender address looks unusual.

Explain why this is phishing rather than just a general scam, and give one safe action Alex should take.

Think about: pretending to be trusted · login information · Pause · Check · Verify · Report

Q10. Malware + Data Interception (3 marks)

Priya downloads a free editing program from an unknown website. After opening it, saved files are copied from her laptop. Later, an attacker also copies data while it travels across a public network.

Identify the TWO threats and explain one risk caused by either threat.

Q11. Weak Password + Identity Theft (3 marks)

Jordan uses the same password for school email, gaming and social media. One account is compromised. Later, someone logs into Jordan's social media and sends messages while pretending to be Jordan.

Explain the password problem, what happened next, and one suitable protection.
